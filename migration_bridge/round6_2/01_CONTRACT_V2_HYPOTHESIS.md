# Independent Contract V2 hypothesis

Deferred execution deliberately changes the layer at which a selected CPU
expert contribution becomes visible. Absolute cross-path margin movement is
therefore not interchangeable with adverse movement toward an anchor flip.

V2 separates (1) exact implementation and no-exposure controls, (2) adverse
contraction and stable/ambiguous decisions, (3) distributional global sanity,
and (4) downstream task quality. Beneficial margin expansion is not itself a
decision failure, but remains subject to numerical sanity checks.

This does not retroactively pass V1, guarantee that V2 will pass, or excuse an
unknown implementation defect. The fixed historical cases are development
evidence only. New QD2 derives numerical reserves; new HD2 can reject them.

The detailed, preregistered method is `CONTRACT_V2_SPEC.md`, first committed in
`61e878739`. No numerical R_D or approved contract exists yet. In particular,
the method uses 95% prompt-block coverage and a separately tested 5% tail-rate
budget; it must never be described as 95% token agreement or as a 99.9% bound.

The experiment remains D1 only. Same placement, runtime, model and artifacts
are retained. Shared NPU0 execution is allowed by the user; interference and
errors must be recorded, with cleanup limited to owned test process groups.
